About CompoundClear

A small library of plain-English explainers on how money and investing actually work — the mechanics behind the slogans, written for a general global audience rather than any one country's tax or legal system.

A lot of personal-finance content lands in one of two places: oversimplified slogans ("just buy index funds and chill") or dense technical writing that assumes you already know the vocabulary. CompoundClear tries to sit in the middle — explaining the actual structure behind compounding, fund types, currency costs, and the rules of thumb that get repeated without much explanation, in plain language, without skipping the parts that make them true.

Because the mechanics of compounding, fund structures, and currency markups work broadly the same way across countries, this site sticks to general, structural explanations rather than country-specific tax or legal detail — the kind of thing that changes by jurisdiction and goes stale fast. Everything here is general education, not personalized advice, and not tied to any bank, fund manager, platform, or broker. Nothing on this site should be read as a recommendation to buy, sell, or hold any specific financial product — for decisions specific to your situation, speak with a licensed financial advisor or tax professional in your own country.
