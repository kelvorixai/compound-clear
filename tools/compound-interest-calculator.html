How this is calculated

This uses the standard compound-interest formula, compounding monthly, with your monthly contribution added at the end of each month:

  • Growth on your starting amount: starting amount × (1 + rate/12)months
  • Growth on contributions: each monthly contribution compounds for the months remaining after it's added
  • Total interest earned is the projected balance minus everything you actually put in (starting amount + all contributions)

This is a mathematical projection based on a fixed, unchanging interest rate — real investments fluctuate year to year, and this doesn't account for taxes, fees, or inflation. It's a tool for understanding the mechanics of compounding, not a guarantee or a forecast of actual returns.

Frequently asked questions

Does this calculator compound monthly or annually?

Monthly — the standard convention for most savings and investment accounts. The annual rate you enter is divided by 12 and applied each month.

Is my monthly contribution added before or after that month's interest?

After — each contribution is added at the end of the month, so it starts earning interest the following month rather than the month it's deposited.

How much of my final balance is actually my own money?

Your total contributed amount is the starting amount plus every monthly contribution added over the period. Everything above that in the projected balance is interest the money earned through compounding.

Does this calculator account for taxes, fees, or inflation?

No — it's a mathematical projection at a fixed, unchanging interest rate. Real investments fluctuate year to year and this tool doesn't adjust for taxes, fees, or inflation.
