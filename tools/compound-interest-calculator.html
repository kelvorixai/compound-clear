How this is calculated

This uses the standard compound-interest formula, compounding monthly, with your monthly contribution added at the end of each month:

  • Growth on your starting amount: starting amount × (1 + rate/12)months
  • Growth on contributions: each monthly contribution compounds for the months remaining after it's added
  • Total interest earned is the projected balance minus everything you actually put in (starting amount + all contributions)

This is a mathematical projection based on a fixed, unchanging interest rate — real investments fluctuate year to year, and this doesn't account for taxes, fees, or inflation. It's a tool for understanding the mechanics of compounding, not a guarantee or a forecast of actual returns.
